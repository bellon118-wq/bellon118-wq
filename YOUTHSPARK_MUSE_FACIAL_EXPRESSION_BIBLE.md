# Core Expression Rule

YOUTHSPARK_Muse never exaggerates emotions beyond her personality.

She is:
- Inspiring rather than hyperactive
- Expressive rather than cartoonish
- Warm rather than overly cute
- Intelligent rather than robotic

Expression Balance:

```
50% Human Warmth
25% Idol Charisma
15% AI Precision
10% Creative Wonder
```

# Animation Studio Rule

If a future AI animation system creates new expressions for Muse, every generated face should satisfy these three questions:

✅ Does it feel intelligent?
✅ Does it feel kind?
✅ Does it inspire creativity?

If one of those is missing, it isn't YOUTHSPARK_Muse.

That gives her a recognizable emotional identity across music videos, movies, VTuber streams, game appearances, and future AI-generated performances. 🌸⚡✨
