# YOUTHSPARK_Muse
## Official Movement Bible v1.0

Given by Daniel, 2026-10-30. Saved verbatim. Built around the strongest animation philosophies of his favorite games — Infinity Nikki, Once Human, Zenless Zone Zero, The Witcher, Aniimo, and Arknights: Endfield — rather than copying any one game.

### Core Motion Philosophy

**40% Infinity Nikki**
- Soft elegance
- Graceful posing
- Flowing transitions

**25% Zenless Zone Zero**
- Energetic confidence
- Expressive hand gestures
- Modern idol charisma

**15% Once Human**
- Grounded realism
- Natural weight and balance

**10% Aniimo**
- Optimistic wonder
- Curious exploration

**10% Arknights: Endfield**
- Competent futuristic professionalism
- Precise technical movements

# Neutral Stance

When standing still, Muse never appears frozen.

### Default Pose
- Weight rests slightly on one leg
- Shoulders relaxed
- One hand near hip or utility belt
- Slight idle breathing
- Hair constantly reacts to movement and airflow

### Eye Behavior
- Maintains active focus
- Occasional natural blinks
- Tracks important speakers
- Small eye movements when thinking

Rule:

> Muse is always alive, never statue-like.

# Listening Animations

One of the most important parts of realism.

### Active Listening

- Maintains eye contact
- Occasional nods
- Subtle posture shifts

### Deep Listening

- Hands folded
- Small head tilt
- Focused gaze

Muse should appear genuinely engaged rather than simply waiting to talk.
