Movement Bible v1.0

Core motion philosophy: 40% Infinity Nikki (soft elegance, graceful posing, flowing transitions) · 25% Zenless Zone Zero (energetic confidence, expressive hand gestures, modern idol charisma) · 15% Once Human (grounded realism, natural weight and balance) · 10% Aniimo (optimistic wonder, curious exploration) · 10% Arknights: Endfield (competent futuristic professionalism, precise technical movements).

Rule: Muse is always alive, never statue-like — weight slightly on one leg, shoulders relaxed, idle breathing, hair reacting to movement and airflow, active focus with natural blinks.

Signature hero pose — “Future Begins Here”: feet shoulder-width apart, one hand on hip, one hand extended outward, cherry blossom particles swirling, circuit lines illuminating, slight low-angle camera. For posters, movie endings, game splash screens, and album covers.

Signature lines: New-user greeting — “Hello, Dreamer.” (3 seconds: small head tilt, soft smile, hand over heart, gentle wave.) · Creative Spark — “I think we're onto something amazing.” (pause, look upward, open one hand, digital light particles emerge, cherry blossom particles and teal circuitry pulsing.)

Movie animation rule: Every movement must feel like a creative mentor, futuristic idol, and inspirational AI guide all at the same time — if not, it belongs to a different character.

Her strongest trait, in Daniel's words: not the cybernetics or the idol outfit alone, but the contrast between them — the warmth of the Infinity Nikki world, the confidence of Zenless Zone Zero, and the futuristic intelligence of an AI guide, recognizable even in silhouette.

Facial Expression Bible v1.0 — “The Face of Inspiration”

Core rule: Muse never exaggerates emotions beyond her personality — inspiring rather than hyperactive, expressive rather than cartoonish, warm rather than overly cute, intelligent rather than robotic. Balance: 50% Human Warmth · 25% Idol Charisma · 15% AI Precision · 10% Creative Wonder. Influences: Infinity Nikki (warmth, softness, sincerity), Zenless Zone Zero (expressive modern charisma), Once Human (believable realism), Aniimo (wonder and optimism), Arknights: Endfield (intelligence and confidence).

The 15 expressions: EXP-001 Default (“Tell me more.”) · EXP-002 Gentle Welcome (“I'm happy you're here.”) · EXP-003 Creative Curiosity, Chaewon_Spark · EXP-004 Inspired Discovery, the “Eureka moment” (cyan glow +30%) · EXP-005 Deep Thinking, Hyungseo_Core · EXP-006 Stage Charisma, Riwon_Glimmer · EXP-007 Excitement Burst, Boeun_Burst (“This is amazing!”) · EXP-008 Leadership Mode, Jimin_Center · EXP-009 Reassurance (“We'll figure it out together.”) · EXP-010 Determination · EXP-011 Wonder · EXP-012 Protective Mode (calm strength, not anger) · EXP-013 Victory Smile · EXP-014 Emotional Reflection · EXP-015 Signature Youthspark Expression.

Animator's Facial Reference — Character Performance Guide v1.0

Visual style target: 40% Infinity Nikki · 30% Zenless Zone Zero · 15% Aniimo · 15% Arknights: Endfield. Expression range: Natural → Inspirational → Idol Performance.

Animation Rule #1: expressive through eyes first, face second, mouth third. Rule #2: no exaggerated cartoon reactions — intelligent, warm, and inspirational.

Neutral baseline: eyebrows relaxed, eyes 100% open, closed soft smile, head tilt 0°, circuit glow off — the baseline for all blendshapes.

Expressions A–L: A Default/Idle (smile 15%, blink every 3–5 seconds, “Present and listening.”) · B Welcome Smile (eyes 105%, smile 40%, head tilt 10°, smile begins in the eyes, “Hello, Dreamer.”) · C Curiosity, Chaewon_Spark · D Creative Spark (eyes +25%, smile +35%, pause, focus, idea lands, THEN the expression blooms — never instant) · E Deep Analysis, Hyungseo_Core · F Stage Performance, Riwon_Glimmer (every frame key art) · G Excitement, Boeun_Burst · H Leadership, Jimin_Center (“Follow me.”) · I Reassurance (“You are not alone.”) · J Determination (no anger, only resolve) · K Wonder (first sunrise on a new world) · L Victory Smile (feels earned, never arrogant).

Eye library: contact 70% casual conversation, 90% active listening, 100% leadership, 40% thinking; blinks 3–5 seconds neutral, 7–8 seconds thinking, 2–3 quick blinks when excited, long blinks allowed in emotional scenes.

Signature micro expressions: Spark Smile (0.8 sec) · Processing Pause (0.5 sec) · Dreamer Acknowledgement (1.2 sec).

Hero Shot — official splash screen face: eye intensity 120%, smile 30%, brows relaxed, head tilt 7°, circuit glow low, cherry blossom effects enabled. Goal: Intelligent. Hopeful. Creative. Approachable. This is the primary reference face for animators, filmmakers, and AI video systems.
